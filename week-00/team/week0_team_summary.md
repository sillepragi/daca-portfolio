# Team Summary: Week 0 — Onboarding and database setup
**Department:** UrbanStyle Marketing analytics department

## Biggest surprise:
The team was surprised that the company has been able to function at all until now, given the current state of "data chaos". Additionally, the introduction of the team charter and the process of creating its template was an unexpected but structured start to our collaboration.

## Conclusion:
The company's data is completely scattered and resides predominantly in Excel, which means the business is currently unable to fully grasp or utilize its own information.

## Decision:
We must migrate to a unified Supabase cloud database. This will end data loss and allow the entire team to work within a single, up-to-date information field, ensuring everyone sees the same "single source of truth".

## Recommendations:
We have established shared tools, assigned specific roles, and jointly discussed the execution of tasks for all team members. We strongly recommend the official adoption of Supabase, GitHub, and NotebookLM as the company’s core environments to simplify and streamline all future data operations.
