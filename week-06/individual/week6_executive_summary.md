### Executive summary: Pärnu store

This report provides an overview of the latest sales results and customer behavior patterns of UrbanStyle.ltd Pärnu store to support a data-driven strategy for the upcoming investor meeting. The focus of the analysis is to identify growth sources and mitigate critical risk areas.

**Key finding no. 1:** The Pärnu store is not just a seasonal summer business for UrbanStyle, but represents a year-round sales unit with high stability and low risk in the company's portfolio.

**Evidence:** The analysis confirms that the difference in turnover between the summer (29%) and winter periods (26%) is only a minimal 3%. This proves that the Pärnu store maintains its viability also outside the tourist season.

**Recommendation:** I recommend maintaining a consistent inventory level and constant staff resources in the Pärnu unit throughout the year, rather than reducing campaigns during the winter period. The Pärnu model is an example of how to mitigate seasonality risks and ensure stable cash flow throughout the financial year.

---

**Key finding no. 2:** UrbanStyle's hit products, or "hero" products, are the company's year-round sales engines, whose potential is underutilized outside the summer season.

**Evidence:** Although the company's current annual growth is 4%, sales analysis shows that hit products dominate sales especially outside the summer season. This suggests that these products hold the key to overcoming slumps and accelerating growth.

**Recommendation:** Increase the winter marketing focus and budget specifically for the *"hero"* product category to increase the current moderate sales growth and maximize revenue during the period when customers prefer basic products.

---

**Key finding no. 3:** The store serves a segment with very high purchasing power, which means that customers prefer quality.

**Evidence:** The average purchase value (AOV) is €273, which is significantly higher than the company’s usual level and indicates customers’ willingness to spend on quality products.

**Recommendation:** Invest more boldly in more exclusive and expensive collections to maximize revenue from this valuable segment.

---

**Key finding no. 4:** Regular dips in sales revenue occur at the beginning of the year and in the fall, indicating the need to improve seasonal stability.

**Evidence:** The data shows that January and October sales numbers are significantly below the annual average, creating revenue gaps that affect the company’s overall cash flow.

**Recommendation:** Plan more aggressive campaigns for these periods to maintain sales momentum even outside the peak season.

---

**Key Finding no. 5:** The integrity of the 2025 data is in a critical state, making adequate business planning and forecasting impossible at this time.

**Evidence:** A 99% decline in revenue has been identified in the 2025 data.

**Recommendation:** The cause of the data loss must be identified and the data must be restored to ensure reliable forecasting capabilities for the upcoming period.
